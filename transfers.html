Common questions

Questions visitors ask us.

Tell us if yours is not here.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book so the pick-up can be timed.

Can I book a transfer on its own?

Yes. A transfer does not have to be part of a safari or a stay.

How long do journeys take?

Distances in Kenya are long, and road times change with traffic and weather. We give you an estimate for your route when we quote.

Need a transfer?
