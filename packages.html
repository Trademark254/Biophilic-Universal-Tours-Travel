What a package covers

What is usually included.

Every quotation lists exactly what is and is not covered, so there are no surprises.

Usually included

Stay, transport and a guide

Lodge or camp nights, a vehicle with a driver guide, park fees and the meals named in the quotation.

Usually extra

Flights and personal costs

International flights, visas, travel insurance, drinks and tips are paid separately unless the quotation says otherwise.

Can be added

Experiences and upgrades

Sundowners, a balloon ride, a village visit, a longer stay or a better room can be added to any trip.
