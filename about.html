About us

A Kenyan travel company built around living things.

Biophilic Universal Tours & Travels Ltd plans safaris, coast stays, car hire and transfers across Kenya. One team plans your route, your stay and your vehicle, and sends you one clear quotation.

Our name

Why Biophilic

Biophilia means a love of living things. We bring it to trip planning by leaving room to watch, walk and sit still.

One team

One planner, one quotation

You deal with one planner for the route, the stay and the vehicle. Every quotation lists what is included, what costs extra and the terms that apply.

How we work

Time in each place

Fewer stops, longer looks

We suggest fewer parks per trip, so days have long drives and no rushed transfers.

Guides

Guides who know the ground

We ask lodges and drivers for guides familiar with the park you are visiting.

Vehicles

Private vehicles

One vehicle per group keeps the day quiet and the pace in your hands.

Impact

Questions about impact
