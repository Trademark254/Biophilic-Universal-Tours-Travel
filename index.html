How we plan

Fewer stops, longer looks.

Biophilia means a love of living things. We apply it to trip planning by leaving room to watch, walk and sit still.

Time in each place

We suggest fewer parks per trip, so days have long drives and no rushed transfers.

Guides who know the ground

We ask lodges and drivers for guides familiar with the park you are visiting.

Private vehicles

One vehicle per group keeps the day quiet and the pace in your hands.

Questions about impact

On request we ask each lodge about water, waste and local hiring, and pass on what they tell us.
