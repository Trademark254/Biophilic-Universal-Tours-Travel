7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • give you a copy of it, including in a usable format where the law allows;
  • correct data that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete data we no longer need;
  • stop using your data for a purpose you object to.

You can also withdraw any consent you gave us. Doing so does not affect what we did before you withdrew it. To use any of these rights, write to info@biophilictours.com. We may need to confirm who you are first.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya at www.odpc.go.ke. We would like the chance to put things right first.

8. Security

We limit who can see personal data, use secure services for storage and email, and ask our suppliers to protect what we send them. No system is completely secure, so please do not send card numbers or passwords by email or WhatsApp.

9. Children

This site is for adults planning travel. If a booking includes children, we collect only the details the trip needs, and we ask a parent or guardian to provide them.
