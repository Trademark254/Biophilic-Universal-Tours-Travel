In detail

Four kinds of stay.

Each suits a different kind of trip. We help you choose.

Lodges

Safari lodges

Permanent buildings in or near a park, usually with a restaurant, a bar and a viewing area. Many offer meals included, so you can go straight from a game drive to a table.

Good for first-time visitors.

Tented camps

Tented camps

Large tents with proper beds and bathrooms, set close to the wildlife. You can hear the animals at night. Facilities range from simple to luxurious.

Good for travellers who want to feel part of the park.

Beach hotels

Beach hotels

Rooms by the sea along the Kenyan coast. A good place to rest at the end of a safari, or a holiday in its own right.

Seasons and sea conditions vary, so we advise for your dates.

BnBs

BnBs and apartments

Rooms and self-catering homes in towns and at the coast, with space to cook and spread out. A good fit for families, groups and longer stays.

House rules and what is provided differ by property.

Common questions

Questions visitors ask us.

Tell us if yours is not here.

What is included in the price?

This differs by place. Many safari lodges include meals, while a BnB may include only the room. Your quotation says what is included and what costs extra.

Can you find something near a park gate?

Yes. Staying close to the gate saves driving time and lets you start your game drive early. Tell us which park you plan to visit.

Do you have family rooms or rooms for groups?

Many places do. Tell us how many adults and children you are, and we match the rooms.

What if I have a dietary or access need?

Tell us when you ask. We check with the place before you confirm.

When should I book?

As early as you can, especially for June to October and for school holidays, when popular places fill up.

Looking for a place to stay?

Tell us where and when, and we will reply with options.
