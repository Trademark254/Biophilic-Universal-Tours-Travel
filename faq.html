Common questions

Questions visitors ask us.

Tell us if yours is not here.

Do I need a visa?

Most visitors need an electronic travel authorisation, which you apply for online before you travel. Rules differ by nationality and change from time to time, so check the official Kenyan government site before you book flights.

Do I need vaccinations or malaria tablets?

Requirements depend on where you travel from and where you go in Kenya. Ask your doctor or a travel clinic well before the trip, and check the official health advice for your country.

What should I pack?

Light layers in neutral colours, a hat, sunscreen, comfortable shoes, binoculars and a camera with spare memory. Mornings and evenings can be cool in the parks, and Mount Kenya is cold at night, so bring a warm layer. A light rain jacket helps in the rainy months.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

Your enquiry opens in WhatsApp. We reply, check availability, adjust the trip with you and send one clear quotation. You confirm only when you are happy with it. No payment is taken on this website.

Can I change a tour package?

Yes. The packages are starting points. Tell us your dates, your group and what you want to change, and we adjust the route, the stay and the vehicle.

Is a safari safe?

Safaris are a normal part of life in Kenya and millions of people take them. Stay with your driver guide, keep to the vehicle where they ask you to, and do not approach wildlife. Check your own government's travel advice before you go.

Can children come on safari?

Many families travel with children. Some lodges and activities set age limits, so tell us the ages when you enquire and we will plan around them.

What money and plugs do I need?

The Kenyan shilling is the local currency. Cards work in cities and larger hotels, and small cash is useful elsewhere. Mobile money is widely used locally. Sockets are the three pin UK type at 240 volts.

Do I need to tip?

Tipping is customary for guides, drivers and camp staff but not compulsory. Ask us and we will explain what is usual for your trip.

What languages are spoken?

English and Swahili are both widely spoken, and our team communicates with guests in English.
