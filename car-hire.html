In detail

What to know before you hire.

Whichever vehicle you pick, a few things apply to driving in Kenya.

Saloon car

Saloon cars

Suited to tarmac roads in Nairobi, along the coast and between the main towns. A saloon is not made for rough park tracks or muddy roads.

Safari van

Safari vans

Roofs that lift for wildlife viewing and seats for several travellers. The usual choice for a road safari with a driver.

Four-wheel drive

Four-wheel-drive vehicles

Higher off the ground, with the grip needed for park tracks, rough roads and wet weather.

On the road

Driving in Kenya

Traffic keeps to the left. Distances are long and journeys take longer than the map suggests. Fuel stops can be far apart in remote areas, and in the parks there are speed limits and rules about staying on the tracks.
